# Founder narrative sourcing

All personal source material for the launch story, pitch decks, and presentations lives in the founder's own project files under `attached_assets/` (narrative arc, profile, milestone timeline, dated journal files). None of it is duplicated into agent memory.

**Why:** memory must never hold personal, financial, or employment details; the founder's files are the single source of truth and stay under his control.

**How to apply:**
- When drafting launch posts, pitch material, or presentations, read the relevant `attached_assets/` files at that moment (glob for `founder-*`, `*-milestone-timeline*`, `*narrative*`).
- Use only content the founder has marked as the approved public arc; private chapters stay private.
- Never copy figures, dates of employment, or personal details from those files into memory or into generated artifacts beyond what the approved arc allows.
