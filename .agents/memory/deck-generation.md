---
name: Slide deck generation
description: Conventions and pitfalls for building Dime Time .pptx decks (Google Slides import)
---

Decks are generated by python-pptx scripts kept next to their output in `attached_assets/<name>-slides/` (patent, pitch, timeline decks all follow this).

**How to verify:** render with `soffice --headless --convert-to pdf` then `pdftoppm -png` and visually inspect every slide — text overflow/wrap bugs are invisible otherwise.

**Pitfalls learned:**
- Emoji in shapes render as tofu boxes in LibreOffice and are unreliable across viewers — use colored oval shapes + text labels instead.
- Long words in narrow shapes wrap mid-word ("MERCURY" → "MERCUR Y"); size fonts to fit the widest word.
- 40pt titles longer than ~28 chars wrap and collide with body text below; pass a smaller title size for long titles.
- Unicode ✓/✗/→ are safe; partner logos are NOT allowed (locked project rule) — names + marks only.

**Brand spec:** lavender #918EF4, deep #4A468F, dark #2D2A4A, light #F5F4FE; Poppins font (in Google Fonts, renders natively after Slides import); transparent logo at `attached_assets/patent-deck-slides/_logo_transparent.png`; 16:9 (13.333×7.5 in).

**Fact anchors for the timeline story:** Sep 19 2025 → Jun 29 2026 = 283 days; Aug 3 2025 → Jul 15 2026 = 346 days (not 347); rejection count is seven (Apple, Sila, Plaid, Axos, Brex + 2 banks); patent is a provisional DRAFT, not filed — don't claim "filed" unless Tim confirms.
