FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 char max — ~1,900 used)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Get out of debt, one dime at a time.

Dime Time turns your everyday spending into steady progress
against your debt. Connect an eligible account, and every
purchase is rounded up to the next dollar — that spare change
accumulates and goes toward paying down the debts you choose,
on a schedule you approve.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Add your debts — credit cards, student loans, auto loans,
   personal loans, or anything else you're paying off
2. Connect an eligible funding account
3. Everyday purchases are rounded up to the next dollar
4. Spare change accumulates automatically
5. You control the destination, schedule, and every payment

KEY FEATURES
• Debt tracking with payoff projections — see your actual
  debt-free date and how much interest you'll save
• Automatic round-ups with optional multipliers (2x, 3x) to
  accelerate your progress
• Payment scheduling you control — money never moves without
  your authorization
• Secure bank connections through regulated financial
  infrastructure partners
• PIN lock and biometric unlock to protect your financial data
• Progress analytics that show exactly where you stand

BUILT FOR SECURITY
• Bank credentials are never stored on your device or our
  servers — connections use tokenized, encrypted access
• All data is encrypted in transit
• PIN and biometric app lock with automatic locking
• Delete your account and data at any time, right in the app

Dime Time is a financial technology platform and is not a bank.
Banking services and payment infrastructure are provided
through regulated financial partners.

Questions? Reach us at tim@dime-time.com or dime-time.com.
