DIME TIME — LONG-TERM PRODUCT VISION & STRATEGIC ROADMAP
(Founder Vision — July 2026)

PURPOSE

Dime Time's mission is to help millions of people improve their financial lives through automation. The core philosophy remains the same:

"Every purchase becomes an opportunity to build a stronger financial future."

The long-term vision is to evolve Dime Time from a debt-payoff application into a complete financial ecosystem.

------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 1 — CORE PRODUCT (CURRENT)
------------------------------------------------------------

✅ Round up debit card purchases
✅ Automatically apply spare change toward debt
✅ Plaid bank connections
✅ Stripe ACH transfers
✅ Live on the Apple App Store
✅ Subscription-based business model

Goal:
Become the easiest and most automated way to pay off debt.

------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 2 — CRYPTO EXPANSION
------------------------------------------------------------

Allow users to choose where their round-ups go.

Options:

• 100% Debt
• 100% Bitcoin
• Split between Debt + Bitcoin

Future exchange integrations:

• Coinbase API
• Kraken API
• Crypto.com API

Users choose their preferred exchange while Dime Time manages automated round-up investing.

------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 3 — MULTI-DESTINATION ROUND UPS
------------------------------------------------------------

Expand beyond a single destination.

Examples:

• 50% Debt
• 30% Bitcoin
• 20% Savings

or

• 40% Debt
• 30% Bitcoin
• 30% Emergency Fund

Allow users complete flexibility over how every round-up is allocated.

------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 4 — DIME TIME WALLET
------------------------------------------------------------

Integrate wallet functionality (potentially through Sila or another compliant wallet provider).

Features:

• Digital wallet
• Store funds
• Receive funds
• Hold balances
• View wallet history
• Wallet integrated directly into the Dime Time dashboard

This becomes the financial hub inside the application.

------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 5 — PEER-TO-PEER PAYMENTS
------------------------------------------------------------

Allow users to send money instantly between Dime Time wallets.

Examples:

• Send $5
• Send $20
• Pay a friend back
• Split dinner
• Birthday gifts

Similar experience to:

• Venmo
• Cash App
• Zelle

Potential revenue:

• Small transaction fees where appropriate
• Premium transfer options
• Additional subscription value

------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 6 — DIME TIME TOKEN (LONG-TERM EXPLORATION)
------------------------------------------------------------

Explore whether a blockchain-based Dime Time token could add value to the ecosystem.

Conceptual goals include:

• Utility within the Dime Time platform
• Wallet support
• Customer ownership and engagement
• Potential future exchange listings if all technical, legal, and regulatory requirements are met

This would require extensive legal and regulatory planning before implementation.

------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 7 — FINANCIAL INTELLIGENCE
------------------------------------------------------------

Use customer-permissioned financial data to create personalized insights.

Examples:

• Debt payoff projections
• Interest saved
• Spending trends
• Financial health scores
• AI-powered recommendations
• Personalized budgeting

Give users a complete picture of their financial progress.

------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 8 — DIME TIME LENDING
------------------------------------------------------------

Once enough customer history exists, explore consumer lending products.

Example:

Customer has:

• 720 credit score
• 18 months of successful Dime Time history
• Excellent payment behavior

Dime Time could present refinancing opportunities.

Example:

Current credit card:

26% APR

Potential refinance:

10–12% APR

Goal:

Reduce interest costs while simplifying debt repayment.

This would require appropriate lending partnerships and regulatory compliance.

------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 9 — CREDIT PRODUCTS
------------------------------------------------------------

Eventually explore:

• Dime Time Credit Card
• Balance transfers
• Rewards tied to debt reduction
• Smart payment automation

Create a credit product aligned with Dime Time's financial wellness mission.

------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 10 — GLOBAL EXPANSION
------------------------------------------------------------

After proving success in the United States:

Potential markets:

• Canada
• United Kingdom
• Australia
• Europe

Expand country by country while adapting to local banking and regulatory requirements.

------------------------------------------------------------
LONG-TERM BUSINESS MODEL
------------------------------------------------------------

Potential revenue streams:

• Monthly subscriptions
• Crypto integrations
• Wallet services
• Peer-to-peer transfers
• Lending partnerships
• Credit products
• Premium financial tools
• International expansion

------------------------------------------------------------
LONG-TERM OBJECTIVE
------------------------------------------------------------

Build Dime Time into one of the world's leading financial wellness platforms.

The vision is to create a company that helps millions of people:

• Eliminate debt
• Build wealth
• Invest automatically
• Move money seamlessly
• Make smarter financial decisions

If successfully executed over the next 3–5 years with millions of active users, recurring subscription revenue, strong customer retention, and a mature product ecosystem, Dime Time could become a strategic acquisition candidate for major fintech companies, payment networks, or financial institutions—or continue operating independently as a scaled global fintech platform.

The focus remains execution: build one exceptional product at a time, earn customer trust, and expand deliberately as each new capability proves its value.
