FOUNDER DECISIONS

I approve the following:

1. ACH PROVISIONAL ACCESS

Set:

SUBSCRIPTION_PROVISIONAL_ACH_DAYS=7

A qualifying ACH subscription may receive a maximum of seven calendar days of provisional access only when the server independently verifies:

* Approved $2.99 Dime Time Debt price
* Correct authenticated user/customer ownership
* Matching subscription, invoice, and PaymentIntent
* us_bank_account payment method
* PaymentIntent status of processing
* Valid mandate
* Server-persisted provisionalAccessUntil
* No failure, cancellation, refund, return, reversal, or dispute

An ordinary `incomplete` subscription must never grant access.

Provisional access must expire automatically after seven calendar days unless Stripe confirms successful payment.

2. PAST-DUE GRACE PERIOD

Set:

SUBSCRIPTION_PAST_DUE_GRACE_DAYS=14

I approve the existing 14-day grace period.

Requirements:

* Grace starts only once on active → past_due.
* Duplicate or out-of-order webhooks cannot extend it.
* Access ends automatically at grace expiration.
* unpaid, canceled, revoked, refunded, or expired subscriptions receive no entitlement.
* A later successful payment may restore active access through authoritative Stripe state.
