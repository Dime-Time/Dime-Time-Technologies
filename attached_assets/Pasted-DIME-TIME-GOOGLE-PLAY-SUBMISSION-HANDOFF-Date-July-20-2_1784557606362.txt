SIGNING SAFETY
================================================================

Google Play signing credentials are critical long-term assets.

Before creating or configuring signing:

- Explain whether Google Play App Signing will be used
- Explain the difference between the upload key and app-signing key
- Identify where the upload key will be stored
- Ensure the key is backed up securely
- Never commit a keystore to GitHub
- Never commit passwords to source control
- Never print signing passwords in logs
- Never ask Tim to paste signing secrets into chat

Add keystore files and local signing-property files to .gitignore before they
are created or used.

Do not rotate, replace, or delete a signing key without explicit founder
authorization.
