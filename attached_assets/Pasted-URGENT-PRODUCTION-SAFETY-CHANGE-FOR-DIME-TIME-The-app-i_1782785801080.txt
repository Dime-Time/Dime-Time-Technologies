2. Add a server-side real-transfer allowlist.
   Preferred:
   - Add column users.realTransfersEnabled boolean default false

   - Stripe bank account/payment method is active and linked;
   - debtId belongs to the logged-in user;
   - amount is positive and within a conservative production limit;
   - user has ACH authorization/mandate on file;
   - idempotency key is required;
   - no duplicate pending transfer exists for the same user/debt/idempotency key.
