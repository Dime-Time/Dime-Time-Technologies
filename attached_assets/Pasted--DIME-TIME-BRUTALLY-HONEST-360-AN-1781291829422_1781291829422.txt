1. PRODUCT
================================================================

STRENGTHS:
- Core mechanic is elegant: split one round-up across multiple
  financial goals simultaneously
- Slider-based allocation UX is intuitive
- Weekly sweep architecture reduces user friction
- Addresses a real psychological problem: competing financial
  priorities with no easy automation tool

WEAKNESSES:
- Round-up math problem: median user generates ~$30-60/month
  in round-ups. Split 3 ways = $10-20 per destination.
  Progress feels invisible, especially on debt. Churn risk.
- No progress amplification layer (multipliers, lump-sum boosts,
  or visualization engine to make micro-progress feel real)
- No defined handling for failed sweeps / NSF events for users
  living paycheck to paycheck (the core target market)
- No built-in virality or sharing mechanic identified

BLIND SPOTS:
- What happens when the bank balance is too low for a sweep?
- How does the app handle disputed transactions or ACH failures?
- Is there a "debt payoff date" countdown or finish line?

OPPORTUNITIES:
- Add visible debt payoff countdown clock
- Add milestone celebration moments designed to be shared
- Add round-up multiplier (2x, 3x) as premium feature

4. COMPLIANCE
================================================================

THIS IS THE HIGHEST-RISK DIMENSION.

LIKELY REGULATORY EXPOSURE:
- Aggregating bank data + initiating ACH transfers + purchasing
  crypto on behalf of users may constitute money transmission
- FinCEN MSB (Money Services Business) registration may be required
- 49 states have separate money transmitter license requirements
- Bitcoin purchases on behalf of users may trigger SEC/FINRA
  broker-dealer or investment advisor considerations
- CFPB oversight applies to consumer financial products
- Regulation E (Electronic Fund Transfer Act) applies to ACH:
  * 60-day dispute window
  * Error resolution procedures required
  * Unauthorized transaction liability rules

COINBASE INTEGRATION QUESTIONS:
- Are users getting custodial accounts?
- Is Dime Time acting as a crypto broker?
- Who holds the Bitcoin on behalf of users?

RECOMMENDED COMPLIANCE ARCHITECTURE:
- Partner with a BaaS provider (Unit, Treasury Prime, Column Bank)
  to hold licenses and provide regulated infrastructure
- Do NOT attempt to obtain money transmitter licenses independently
  at this stage

IMMEDIATE ACTION:
- Retain a fintech compliance attorney NOW
- Get a formal legal opinion on MSB status before scaling
- Review Apple App Store financial app requirements carefully
