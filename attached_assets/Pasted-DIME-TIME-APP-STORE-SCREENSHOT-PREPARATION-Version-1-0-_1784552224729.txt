================================================================
APPLE SCREENSHOT REQUIREMENTS
================================================================

Apple currently permits:

- Minimum: 1 screenshot per required device class
- Maximum: 10 screenshots
- Formats: PNG, JPEG, or JPG
- No alpha channel/transparency

Apple automatically scales the highest-resolution screenshots down for smaller
device sizes when the interface is the same.

IPHONE 6.9-INCH ACCEPTED PORTRAIT SIZES

Apple currently accepts these portrait dimensions for the 6.9-inch set:

- 1260 × 2736
- 1290 × 2796
- 1320 × 2868

The exact output depends on the selected Pro Max/Plus simulator.

IPAD 13-INCH ACCEPTED PORTRAIT SIZES

Apple accepts:

- 2064 × 2752
- 2048 × 2732

The 13-inch iPad screenshot set is required when the app runs on iPad.

Before uploading, verify each file’s pixel dimensions in Finder:

1. Select the screenshot.
2. Press Command-I.
3. Review “Dimensions.”

Do not resize or stretch screenshots unless necessary. Prefer direct simulator
captures at an accepted size.
