DEFINE METRICS BEFORE PUBLIC LAUNCH
================================================================

Create exact reporting definitions now so future investor metrics
remain consistent, auditable and defensible.

ACTIVATED USER
Suggested definition:
A registered user who completes the required onboarding steps and
successfully links an eligible bank account.

LINKED ACCOUNT
Suggested definition:
A unique eligible financial account successfully connected and active,
excluding deleted, disconnected, duplicate and sandbox accounts.

PUBLIC USER
Suggested definition:
A genuine non-founder user with a production account. Exclude demo,
reviewer, staff, test and sandbox accounts.

PAYING SUBSCRIBER
Suggested definition:
A user with a successfully paid, currently active subscription. Do not
count free trials, failed payments, complimentary access or canceled
subscriptions.

ACH PAYMENT
Suggested definition:
A unique production ACH payment that has been successfully initiated.
Report processing, settled, failed and returned payments separately.

SETTLED ACH VOLUME
Suggested definition:
The dollar value of production ACH payments that successfully settled,
excluding failed, canceled, reversed and returned payments.

DEBT REDUCED
Suggested definition:
The verified principal amount successfully applied to user debt through
settled Dime Time-facilitated payments. Do not count pending transfers,
interest avoided or projected savings unless reported separately.

ACTIVATION RATE
Suggested definition:
Activated users divided by eligible completed registrations for the
same defined cohort and reporting period.

SUBSCRIPTION CONVERSION
Suggested definition:
New paying subscribers divided by eligible activated users for the same
defined cohort and reporting period.

RETENTION
Suggested definition:
The percentage of a defined activated-user or paying-subscriber cohort
that remains active after a stated interval, such as 30, 60 or 90 days.

CUSTOMER ACQUISITION COST
Suggested definition:
Attributable acquisition spending divided by newly acquired customers
for a clearly defined period and channel. Report registered-user CAC
and paying-customer CAC separately.

MONTH-OVER-MONTH GROWTH
Suggested definition:
The percentage change in a specific metric from one complete calendar
month to the next. Do not combine unlike metrics or use incomplete
months without clearly labeling them.
