We have identified a fundamental product and architecture issue.

My intended Dime Time experience has always been:

1. The user connects their checking account.
2. Plaid provides transaction data used to calculate round-ups.
3. The user’s debts are imported automatically.
4. The user selects one of the imported debts as the payoff target.
5. Dime Time collects or initiates the accumulated round-up amount.
6. The payment is routed automatically to the selected creditor.
7. The user is not required to manually locate or enter a creditor routing number and ACH account number.

Do not treat manual creditor routing-number and account-number entry as the final solution. Many credit cards and liabilities do not expose ordinary ACH deposit coordinates to consumers, and a credit-card account number is not necessarily a valid ACH destination account number.

Plaid Liabilities appears to provide debt data rather than a complete creditor-payment rail. Plaid Auth provides routing and account numbers for eligible checking, savings and cash-management funding accounts, not necessarily payment coordinates for imported liability accounts.
