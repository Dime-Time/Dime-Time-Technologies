UPDATED PRIORITY ORDER
==========================================================

🔴 PRIORITY 1 — VERIFY EMAIL DELIVERY

This remains the highest priority.

Before inviting anyone to test:

- Verify emails arrive quickly.
- Test Gmail.
- Test Outlook.
- Test Yahoo if possible.
- Check spam folders.
- Measure delivery time.
- Confirm users can verify accounts without confusion.

If signup fails, nothing else matters.

==========================================================
🔴 PRIORITY 2 — WATCH A COMPLETE FIRST-TIME USER

Don't only verify email.

Watch ONE coworker complete the ENTIRE production experience without coaching.

The test should include:

• Account creation
• Email verification
• Login
• Plaid bank connection
• Debt import
• Target debt selection
• Round-up configuration
• Dashboard arrival

Do not help unless absolutely necessary.

Observe every hesitation.

Write down:

- Questions they ask
- Places they stop
- Buttons they miss
- Moments of confusion
- Screens they reread
- Anything they expect but don't see

This is the single most valuable usability test before inviting more users.

==========================================================
🔴 PRIORITY 3 — DECIDE THE SUBSCRIPTION POLICY

The biggest trust risk isn't charging $2.99/month.

The biggest trust risk is surprising people.

Be transparent before they reach the paywall.

Suggested wording:

"Thanks for helping me test Dime Time. One thing you'll notice is that automatic round-ups are part of the $2.99/month subscription. I intentionally left it this way because I'd really like your honest opinion about whether the pricing feels fair and whether the experience is worth it. Please don't be polite—if something feels confusing or overpriced, tell me."

This frames the subscription as something being evaluated rather than sold.

==========================================================
🟠 PRIORITY 4 — APP STORE OPTIMIZATION

Before LinkedIn marketing begins:

Review:

• App title
• Subtitle
• Keywords
• Description
• Screenshot order
• Screenshot messaging
• "What's New"
• App Preview video opportunities

The App Store page is the landing page for everyone coming from LinkedIn.

==========================================================
🟠 PRIORITY 5 — HIDE THE CRYPTO TAB

Recommendation:

Temporarily disable the Crypto preview.

Reason:

The launch message should remain extremely simple:

"Dime Time helps people pay off debt automatically."

Introducing Bitcoin at launch creates unnecessary cognitive load and may reduce trust among users whose immediate goal is debt reduction.

Crypto can return later after trust has been established.

==========================================================
WHAT IS MISSING?

I believe one important UX opportunity remains.

==========================================================
1. THE EXPECTATION GAP

After onboarding finishes, users should immediately understand what happens next.

Many people have never used an automated fintech app.

The dashboard should clearly explain:

✓ Bank connected

✓ Debt imported

✓ Target debt selected

✓ Round-ups enabled

✓ Waiting for your next eligible purchase

The final message is especially important.

It tells users that nothing is broken—they simply haven't made another qualifying purchase yet.

==========================================================
2. THE "NOTHING HAPPENED" PROBLEM

Many fintech apps appear inactive immediately after setup.

Avoid this.

Once onboarding finishes, the dashboard should reassure users that the system is actively waiting for their next purchase.

Users should never wonder:

"Did it actually work?"

or

"Is anything happening?"

==========================================================
USER TESTING BEST PRACTICES

Ask open-ended questions before specific questions.

Instead of:

"Was it easy?"

Ask:

"What do you think this screen is asking you to do?"

"What do you think happens if you press this button?"

"What were you expecting to happen?"

These questions reveal far more than satisfaction scores.

==========================================================
DO NOT HELP DURING OBSERVATION

If someone gets stuck...

Stay silent.

Observe.

Only after the task ask:

"What were you expecting?"

The moments where users hesitate are usually the highest-value UX improvements.

==========================================================
SEPARATE BUGS FROM UX

Maintain two lists.

BUGS
- Crash
- Doesn't work
- Import failure
- Incorrect calculations
- Login problems

UX
- Confusing wording
- Didn't notice button
- Didn't trust Plaid
- Didn't know they were finished
- Didn't understand what happened next

These should be prioritized separately.

==========================================================
ASK ONE FINAL QUESTION

At the end of every beta session ask:

"If Dime Time disappeared tomorrow, would you miss it? Why or why not?"

If users say yes...

Learn why.

If users say no...

That answer is equally valuable.
