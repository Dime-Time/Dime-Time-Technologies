DIME TIME — SUBSCRIPTION BILLING IMPLEMENTATION PLAN (July 2026)

GOAL:
Launch Dime Time with simple recurring monthly subscriptions billed
through Stripe Billing (not Apple In-App Purchase, assuming the app
continues to qualify under Apple's financial-services guidelines).
The objective is to begin generating recurring revenue immediately
once users subscribe while keeping the onboarding experience simple.

SUBSCRIPTION TIERS:

1. DIME TIME DEBT
- $2.99/month
- Automatically round up everyday debit purchases
- Apply spare change toward the user's selected debt via ACH payments

2. DIME TIME BITCOIN
- $3.99/month
- Automatically round up purchases
- Purchase Bitcoin using the accumulated round-ups
- Coinbase integration required before launch

3. DIME TIME SPLIT
- $4.99/month
- Automatically split round-ups between:
  • Debt payoff
  • Bitcoin purchases

------------------------------------------------------------

SUBSCRIPTION FLOW:

1. User creates an account.
2. User links their bank account.
3. User selects one of the subscription plans.
4. Present a checkout screen showing:
   - Monthly subscription price
   - Billing frequency
   - Auto-renewal terms
   - Cancellation policy
5. Require the user to:
   - Agree to the Terms of Service
   - Agree to the Privacy Policy
   - Explicitly authorize recurring monthly billing
6. User taps "Subscribe."
7. Stripe creates the subscription.
8. Round-ups begin immediately.

NOTE:
Agreement to the Terms of Service can include recurring billing
language, but the subscription itself should also require explicit,
affirmative consent to recurring charges.

------------------------------------------------------------

BILLING STRATEGY:

Recommended approach:

• Bill each customer based on their individual signup date.

Example:
- July 14: User subscribes
- July 14: First payment (or free trial if offered)
- August 14: Renewal
- September 14: Renewal

Advantages:
- Eliminates billing spikes.
- Easier recurring subscription management.
- Fully supported by Stripe Billing.
- No need to prorate mid-month signups.

Alternative:
A fixed billing date (such as the 15th of every month) is possible
using Stripe Billing Anchors, but requires handling prorated billing
for customers who subscribe between billing cycles.

------------------------------------------------------------

LAUNCH REVENUE STRATEGY:

If the goal is immediate recurring revenue:

- Charge users immediately when they subscribe.
- Do not require a free trial.
- Begin recurring monthly billing immediately.

This produces revenue from the first day customers begin subscribing.

------------------------------------------------------------

CANCELLATION POLICY:

The subscription experience should clearly explain:

- Billing occurs monthly.
- Subscription renews automatically until canceled.
- Users can cancel at any time.
- Cancellation process.
- Refund policy (if applicable).

Clear disclosure reduces customer confusion and minimizes chargebacks.

------------------------------------------------------------

PRODUCT STRATEGY OBSERVATION:

The proposed pricing ladder is simple and easy for users to understand:

$2.99 → Debt
$3.99 → Bitcoin
$4.99 → Split

It creates natural upgrade paths while keeping the core product
accessible.

The primary focus should remain the debt-payoff product first, since
it is already functioning in production. Bitcoin tiers can be enabled
once Coinbase integration is complete.

------------------------------------------------------------

IMPLEMENTATION NOTES FOR REPLIT:

- Integrate Stripe Billing subscriptions.
- Create Products and Prices for all three plans.
- Store the user's selected subscription tier.
- Verify active subscription status before enabling premium features.
- Handle renewals, cancellations, and payment failures via Stripe
  webhooks.
- Require explicit recurring billing authorization during checkout.
- Log acceptance of the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and
  subscription authorization (including timestamp/version) for audit
  purposes.
- Support future plan upgrades and downgrades without requiring users
  to create a new account.

OBJECTIVE:
Keep onboarding friction low while creating predictable monthly
recurring revenue and a scalable subscription model for Dime Time.
