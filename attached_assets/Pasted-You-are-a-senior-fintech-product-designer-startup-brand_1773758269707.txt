PRODUCT CONTEXT

Dime Time is a fintech application that automatically rounds up everyday purchases and allocates spare change toward two specific financial outcomes:

• debt repayment  
• cryptocurrency purchases

Unlike traditional round-up apps that place spare change into savings accounts, Dime Time routes spare change toward reducing liabilities or acquiring digital assets.

The platform allows users to split round-ups dynamically between these two destinations.

Example allocation:

80% → credit card or debt payoff  
20% → cryptocurrency purchases

The goal of the platform is to convert everyday spending into automated financial discipline that either reduces debt or builds digital asset exposure.
