DIME TIME MOBILE — PROJECT SUMMARY
As of June 3, 2026

MISSION
Dime Time helps consumers get out of debt one dime at a time by automatically rounding up everyday purchases and applying those savings toward debt reduction. The platform is designed to combine behavioral finance, automation, debt tracking, and optional future investing tools into a simple mobile experience.
