================================================================

PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Create a polished, educational presentation for Tim Carlisle to use
when speaking at universities, business schools, entrepreneurship
programs and information-systems programs.

This is NOT an investor pitch deck.

The presentation should tell the true story of how a U.S. Navy veteran,
hospitality professional and nontraditional technology founder used his
MBA, graduate education in Management Information Systems, AI-assisted
development and persistent execution to build and launch a real fintech
application.

The audience may include:
- Undergraduate business students
- MBA students
- Information-systems students
- Entrepreneurship students
- Finance and fintech students
- Veterans transitioning into business or technology
- Faculty, administrators and university startup communities

The presentation should be educational, honest, inspiring and grounded
in verified milestones. It should explain both the accomplishments and
the difficult realities of building a regulated financial product.

SLIDE 6 — THE SOLUTION
----------------------------------------------------------------

Title:

Get Out of Debt, One Dime at a Time

Explain the Dime Time concept:

1. A user connects an eligible financial account
2. Everyday purchases are analyzed
3. Each purchase is rounded up
4. Round-ups accumulate
5. The user controls the destination and schedule
6. Authorized ACH payments apply accumulated amounts toward debt

Important accuracy note:

Money does not move after every individual purchase. Round-ups
accumulate and are transferred according to a user-approved schedule.

Include the slogan:

“Get out of debt, one dime at a time.”

SLIDE 14 — SETBACKS AS SYSTEM FEEDBACK
----------------------------------------------------------------

Title:

Every Failure Became a Better Control

Use a table with three columns:

CHALLENGE | RESPONSE | LESSON

Suggested rows:

App Store duplicate-app rejection
| Removed the obsolete record and clarified the production app
| Compliance problems require evidence and precise communication

Production email failures
| Diagnosed delivery paths, corrected notifications and added tests
| A feature is not working merely because the interface says it is

Real-money risk
| Added feature flags, allowlisting, limits and audit controls
| Safety must be designed before activation

OAuth return-flow issues
| Added universal-link and resume handling
| The complete user journey matters, not only the API call

Android permission risk
| Removed permissions the product did not need
| Minimize access and justify every permission

Do not present mistakes as blame. Present them as evidence of learning,
systems thinking and responsible iteration.
