DIME TIME — ALPACA EMBEDDED CRYPTO CONCEPT
Date: July 28, 2026
Purpose: Product and technical briefing for Replit
Status: Discovery only — do not implement yet

==================================================
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
==================================================

Dime Time should continue exploring Alpaca Broker API as a possible
long-term provider for real crypto round-ups.

The proposed experience:

- Dime Time calculates purchase round-ups
- Users choose an optional crypto allocation
- Crypto portions accumulate until reaching a purchase threshold
- Dime Time instructs Alpaca to make the purchase
- Alpaca handles customer accounts, execution and custody
- The user owns the cryptocurrency
- Dime Time displays the account and purchase information through Alpaca APIs

This could create an Acorns-style embedded experience where users buy
crypto inside Dime Time without needing an existing Coinbase account.

IMPORTANT:

This is a product concept under investigation. No real Alpaca integration
should be implemented until Alpaca confirms pricing, funding architecture,
compliance responsibilities and startup eligibility.

==================================================
EXPLAINED SIMPLY
==================================================

Example:

1. The user buys coffee for $4.25.
2. Dime Time rounds the purchase up to $5.00.
3. The total round-up is $0.75.
4. The user selected 20% of round-ups for crypto.
5. Dime Time allocates:
   - $0.60 toward debt
   - $0.15 toward crypto
6. Dime Time continues tracking future crypto allocations.
7. When the crypto allocation reaches a threshold—such as $5—Dime Time
   sends Alpaca a purchase instruction.
8. Alpaca deducts its disclosed transaction fee or spread.
9. Alpaca purchases the selected fractional cryptocurrency.
10. Alpaca holds the crypto in an individually owned custodial account
    for that user.
11. Dime Time receives the transaction details through Alpaca's APIs.
12. Dime Time displays the user's balance and purchase history.

Example purchase:

- Accumulated crypto allocation: $5.00
- Hypothetical Alpaca fee: $0.05
- Crypto purchased: $4.95

The actual fees remain unknown and must be confirmed with Alpaca.

==================================================
RESPONSIBILITY DIVISION
==================================================

DIME TIME WOULD:

- Track eligible transactions
- Calculate purchase round-ups
- Apply the user's debt-versus-crypto allocation
- Accumulate pending crypto allocations in its ledger
- Enforce thresholds and purchase limits
- Send authorized purchase instructions
- Display balances, activity and purchase history
- Provide pause and allocation controls
- Maintain internal audit and reconciliation records
- Prevent duplicate purchase instructions
- Keep debt payoff as the primary product

ALPACA WOULD IDEALLY:

- Create individually owned customer accounts
- Perform customer onboarding and KYC
- Perform sanctions and compliance screening
- Receive or pull the customer's crypto funding
- Execute fractional cryptocurrency purchases
- Custody the purchased cryptocurrency
- Maintain official positions and account records
- Provide transaction and order information
- Provide trade confirmations
- Maintain tax and cost-basis records
- Supply required customer documents

The exact division of responsibility must be confirmed in writing.

THE USER WOULD:

- Choose whether to enable crypto
- Choose the supported cryptocurrency
- Choose the allocation percentage
- Approve the standing purchase instructions
- Own the purchased cryptocurrency
- Be able to pause or disable future crypto purchases

==================================================
IMPORTANT CUSTODY DISTINCTION
==================================================

Dime Time should calculate and track the round-ups, but it should not
physically hold the money designated for crypto.

Dime Time should never:

- Pool customer crypto money
- Receive crypto funds into a company account
- Purchase crypto through the founder's Coinbase account
- Hold customer cryptocurrency
- Store manual exchange API keys supplied by users
- Control a shared customer wallet
- Route Stripe ACH proceeds into crypto without provider approval

The preferred architecture:

- Money flows directly into the user's individually owned Alpaca account
- Alpaca executes the purchase
- Alpaca Crypto holds the assets for the individual user
- Dime Time acts as the software interface and instruction layer

The account may look like a wallet inside Dime Time, but Alpaca would
maintain the official account and custody behind the scenes.

==================================================
FUNDING QUESTION — NOT YET RESOLVED
==================================================

The most important unresolved question is how the $5 reaches Alpaca.

Possible model A:

- The user maintains a cash balance in their Alpaca account
- Dime Time triggers a purchase using the available balance

Possible model B:

- Alpaca pulls the accumulated purchase amount directly from the user's
  linked bank account when the threshold is reached

Possible model C:

- The user periodically funds their Alpaca account manually

Dime Time should prefer Model B if it can be implemented legally,
economically and with proper standing authorization.

Do not assume Dime Time's existing Plaid or Stripe bank connection can
be reused. Alpaca may require users to link or verify their bank again.

The subscription could include:

- Automated debt round-ups

- Crypto threshold controls
- Purchase tracking
- Portfolio display
- Integrated reporting and insights

Important pricing distinction:

- The $3.99 subscription pays Dime Time for its software and automation
- Alpaca's trading spread, funding fee or transaction charge is separate
- All fees must be clearly disclosed
- Dime Time must determine whether to pass through or absorb provider fees

Do not lock the $3.99 price until Alpaca provides:

- Monthly platform minimum
- Setup fee
- Per-account fee
- KYC charges
- ACH funding fees
- Trading fees or spreads
- Custody fees
- Minimum supported order size

If provider costs are high, $3.99 may not be profitable at low volume.

- Alpaca offers Broker API and development resources

- Alpaca appears more attainable than Zero Hash

targets approximately $10–15 million in monthly volume. That is not
appropriate for Dime Time's current stage.
