DIME TIME — COMPLETE MASTER TIMELINE
(Updated through July 14, 2026)

Founder:
Tim Carlisle
U.S. Navy Veteran • MBA + MIS (UNLV) • Certified Sommelier
Founder & CEO, Dime Time Technologies LLC

MISSION
Help consumers eliminate debt automatically by rounding up everyday
debit purchases and applying the spare change toward real debt payments
via ACH.

• Instead of sending spare change to savings, direct it toward debt.

2015–2024
• Multiple concept revisions.
• Early prototype work during MBA/MIS studies at UNLV.
• Initial outreach to banks and investors using only the concept.
• The company survives nearly a decade without funding or a technical
team.
