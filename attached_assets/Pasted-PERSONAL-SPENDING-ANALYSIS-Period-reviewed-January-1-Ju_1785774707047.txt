HONEST ASSESSMENT
==================================================

The concern about spending too much at bars is supported by the statements.

The bar spending is materially affecting overall finances because:

1. It totaled more than $8,100 in approximately six and a half months.
2. It accelerated sharply from March through June.
3. It occurred alongside substantial debt payments.
4. It contributed to repeated overdrafts.
5. It competed with money needed for savings and Dime Time development.
6. One venue, Liquid Zoo, accounted for more than half of the identified nightlife total.

However, the problem is concentrated enough to be addressed. A small number of venues and behaviors account for a large portion of the damage.

==================================================
RECOMMENDED IMMEDIATE ACTIONS
==================================================

1. Take a 30-day break from Liquid Zoo and Stoneleigh.

2. Establish a hard nightlife allowance of $100 per week.

3. Place that allowance in a separate spending account or use cash so it cannot exceed the predetermined limit.

4. Do not permit bar or restaurant spending when checking is below a predetermined safety floor.

5. Turn off overdraft spending where practical and maintain a protected checking buffer.

6. Review every Apple charge and cancel, dispute, or eliminate anything unrecognized or unnecessary.

7. Assign a purpose to every Cash App, Apple Cash, and Zelle transfer.

8. Separate personal spending from Dime Time business spending.

9. Track bars, restaurants, and alcohol as separate categories going forward.

10. Conduct a weekly 15-minute review rather than waiting until the end of the month.
