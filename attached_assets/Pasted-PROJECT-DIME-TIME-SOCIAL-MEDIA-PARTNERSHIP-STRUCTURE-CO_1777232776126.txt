PROJECT: DIME TIME — SOCIAL MEDIA PARTNERSHIP STRUCTURE

CONTEXT:
Greg (PhD, National Social Media Marketing Manager — Boy Scouts of America)
has offered to help launch Dime Time’s social media marketing at no cost.

Founder intent:
Compensate via equity once Dime Time converts to a Delaware C-Corp.

---

CRITICAL RULE:

DO NOT:
- Promise vague “shares later”
- Handshake equity agreements
- Grant equity before company structure is finalized

ALL EQUITY MUST BE:
- Documented
- Vested
- Performance-based
- Approved at incorporation

---

RECOMMENDED STRUCTURE:

1. PRE-CORP PHASE (NOW)

Create a simple written agreement:

TYPE:
Advisor / Contractor Agreement (Pre-Incorporation)

TERMS:
- Compensation: $0 cash
- Future equity: subject to board approval upon incorporation
- No guaranteed ownership today

LANGUAGE:
“Any equity compensation is subject to future board approval
and formal grant following incorporation.”

---

2. POST-CORP STRUCTURE (DELAWARE C-CORP)

Grant via:

EQUITY TYPE:
- Advisor Shares or Stock Options (NOT immediate common stock)

ALLOCATION RANGE:
- 0.25% – 1.0% (typical for early advisor with real contribution)

Use lower end unless:
- He is executing consistently
- Driving measurable user growth

---

3. VESTING (MANDATORY)

Structure:
- 2-year vesting
- Monthly vesting schedule
- No cliff or short cliff (3–6 months optional)

Example:
0.5% total
→ ~0.0208% per month vested

---

4. PERFORMANCE LINKING (IMPORTANT)

Tie equity to outcomes:

EXAMPLES:
- Launch TikTok / Instagram presence
- Achieve first 10K followers
- Drive first 1,000 app downloads
- Maintain weekly content cadence

Avoid:
“Free equity for effort alone”

---

5. ROLE DEFINITION

TITLE:
“Social Media Advisor” or “Growth Advisor”

NOT:
Co-founder (unless truly building company with you)

---

6. DELIVERABLES

Define clearly:

- Content strategy (short-form video focus)
- Posting schedule (daily / weekly cadence)
- Platform focus (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts)
- Growth tracking metrics

---

7. IP OWNERSHIP

All content created:
→ Owned by Dime Time LLC / future C-Corp

---

8. CONVERSION EVENT

When you form C-Corp:
- Convert agreement into formal advisor equity grant
- Issue stock options via standard plan

---

RISKS IF NOT STRUCTURED:

- Cap table disputes
- Misaligned expectations
- Future investor concern (“who owns what?”)
- Difficulty raising capital

---

FOUNDER POSITIONING:

“This is an advisory relationship with performance-based equity
that vests over time once the company is incorporated.”

---

BOTTOM LINE:

You are NOT giving away your company.

You are:
- Trading a small, controlled % of equity
- For execution that directly drives growth
- Under structured, investor-safe terms
