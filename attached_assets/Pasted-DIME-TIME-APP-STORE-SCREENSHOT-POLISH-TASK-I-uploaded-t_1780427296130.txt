Do NOT include:

Dime Time is NOT a bank.
If the app uses the word "Banking", it should only mean connected external accounts / payment settings.
Do not imply Dime Time provides banking services.

5. DEMO DATA
Replace weak/empty values where possible:
- Avoid $0.00 on analytics/insights screens
- Avoid "N/A"
- Use realistic sample values only

Examples:

- Round-up Rate: 3.8%
- Top spending category: Coffee Shops or Dining & Restaurants

"increasing your round-ups by just $1 per transaction could help you pay off debt 6 months faster"

"increasing your round-ups may help you pay down debt faster over time"

Avoid guaranteed financial results.
