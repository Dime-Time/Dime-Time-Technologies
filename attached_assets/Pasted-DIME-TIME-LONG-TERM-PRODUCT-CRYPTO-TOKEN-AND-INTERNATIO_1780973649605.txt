DIME TIME — LONG-TERM PRODUCT, CRYPTO, TOKEN, AND INTERNATIONAL EXPANSION ROADMAP

OVERVIEW

Dime Time's long-term vision should not be limited to being a debt-payoff application.

The larger opportunity is to become a financial allocation platform that automatically directs small amounts of money toward a user's financial goals.

The core philosophy remains:

"Take small amounts of money automatically and move them toward a better financial future."

Debt reduction is the initial use case.

Over time, Dime Time can evolve into a broader financial ecosystem.

## PHASE 7 — INTERNATIONAL EXPANSION

IMPORTANT:

International expansion should occur AFTER proving success in the United States.

Why?

Fintech does not scale internationally as easily as social media.

Each country introduces:

• Banking regulations
• Privacy regulations
• Tax requirements
• Money movement laws
• Identity verification requirements

The product concept is universal.

The infrastructure is not.

## PHASE 9 — DIME TIME TOKEN (DTT)

This should occur ONLY AFTER:

• Successful launch
• Product-market fit
• Revenue generation
• Significant user adoption

Do NOT prioritize token creation before proving market demand.

---

## WHY NOT LAUNCH A TOKEN EARLY?

Launching a token before user adoption creates risk:

• Regulatory complexity
• Legal costs
• Compliance burden
• Distraction from core product growth

The strongest token ecosystems emerge AFTER a successful product exists.
