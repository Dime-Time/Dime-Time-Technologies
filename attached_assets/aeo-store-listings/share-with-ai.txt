----------------------------------------------------------------
Dime Time is a round-up app for paying off debt. It rounds your everyday purchases up to the next dollar and puts the spare change toward your credit cards, student loans, and other debts — with a payoff date you can see and payments that never move without your approval.

Get out of debt, one dime at a time.

HOW DOES DIME TIME WORK?
1. Add your debts — credit cards, student loans, auto loans, personal loans, medical bills, anything you're paying off
2. Connect an eligible funding account
3. Every purchase is rounded up to the next dollar — a $4.30 coffee sets aside $0.70
4. Spare change accumulates automatically; add a 2x or 3x multiplier to go faster
5. You approve the destination, the schedule, and every payment

WHAT MAKES DIME TIME DIFFERENT?
Most round-up apps send your spare change to savings or investing. Dime Time points it at your debt instead, so every dime goes toward cutting the balances — and the interest — you owe.

WHAT CAN I TRACK IN DIME TIME?
• Every debt in one place, with balances and interest rates
• Your projected debt-free date, updated as you pay
• Interest saved by paying ahead of schedule
• Progress analytics that show exactly where you stand

IS DIME TIME SAFE?
• Bank credentials are never stored on your device or our servers — connections use tokenized, encrypted access through regulated financial infrastructure partners
• All data is encrypted in transit
• PIN and biometric app lock with automatic locking
• Delete your account and data at any time, right in the app

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Does rounding up spare change really help pay off debt?
Small amounts add up. A few purchases a day means dimes and quarters accumulating every week, and optional 2x–3x multipliers speed it up. Dime Time shows the effect on your payoff date, so progress is visible instead of theoretical.

Is Dime Time a bank?
No. Dime Time is a financial technology platform, not a bank. Banking services and payment infrastructure are provided through regulated financial partners.

Does Dime Time move money without my permission?
Never. You approve the destination, the schedule, and every individual payment before anything moves.

What debts can I pay off with Dime Time?
Credit cards, student loans, auto loans, personal loans, medical debt — if you're paying it off, you can track it and point your spare change at it.

How much does Dime Time cost?
Dime Time is free to download, and you can see plan details and pricing in the app before committing to anything.

Can I delete my account and data?
Yes — delete your account and all data at any time, right in the app.

Dime Time is a financial technology platform and is not a bank. Banking services and payment infrastructure are provided through regulated financial partners.
