DIME TIME — AUTHENTICATION STRATEGY REVIEW
EMAIL VERIFICATION vs SMS (2FA) VERIFICATION

Context:

While preparing Build 210 for public launch, we've identified the email verification step as one of the largest potential onboarding friction points.

Current authentication flow:

- User creates an account
- Verification email is sent
- User leaves the app
- Opens their email
- Finds the verification message
- Clicks the verification link
- Returns to Dime Time
- Continues onboarding

Our concern is that this may become one of the biggest abandonment points, especially among first-time users.

I'm considering replacing email verification with SMS verification using a one-time passcode (OTP), where the user enters their mobile number and receives a six-digit verification code by text message.

I want you to evaluate this idea based on Dime Time's existing architecture—not as a hypothetical greenfield redesign.
