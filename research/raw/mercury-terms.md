## Key Facts

- **Mercury is a fintech, NOT a bank.** Banking services are provided by partner banks: **Choice Financial Group, Column N.A., and Patriot Bank, N.A.**, all Members FDIC. Mercury historically used **Evolve Bank & Trust** but announced March 2025 it is transitioning away from Evolve. (mercury.com/blog/how-mercury-works-with-partner-banks, dated Mar 11, 2025; support.mercury.com/.../Understanding-FDIC-insurance)
- **Terms of Use §1.4 (Prohibitions) explicitly bans third-party use:** "Use the Account or the Services for any third parties" is a listed prohibited use. Also bans non-commercial use of business accounts. (mercury.com/legal/terms, Last Updated June 25, 2026)
- **Terms of Use §9 "No Money Transmission":** "Mercury does not transmit money or monetary value on your behalf. We do not accept funds from you for the purpose of sending them to another person or entity. Any movement of funds occurs through our partner banks... not through Mercury acting as an intermediary." (mercury.com/legal/terms)
- **Account closure grounds explicitly include third-party fund handling:** Mercury lists "using your Mercury account to facilitate payments for third parties" and "issuing cards to unaffiliated third parties to evade KYC or other compliance requirements" and "using Mercury services to avoid federal or state regulatory obligations" as closure reasons. (support.mercury.com/.../Understanding-account-closures-and-access-restrictions)
- **Mercury does NOT offer FBO/custodial/omnibus accounts to its customers.** Its own blog on DDAs vs. FBOs describes FBO/pooled/omnibus structures generically (a fintech opening an omnibus account at a partner bank with a ledger of sub-accounts), then states: "What kind of account does Mercury provide? Mercury is a fintech company that provides banking services through partner banks. Accounts are structured to give customers direct access to their funds..." — i.e., Mercury provides standard business deposit accounts, not an FBO-for-your-end-users product. (mercury.com/blog/demand-depost-accounts-vs-for-benefit-of-accounts, Aug 1, 2025)
- **Savings/checking:** Deposits held at Choice Financial Group and Column N.A.; via sweep networks eligible for up to $5M FDIC pass-through insurance (conditions apply). Interest on swept balances is based on the rate the Core Bank makes available less fees; account ownership is book-entry by the Administrator. (support FDIC page; Choice Sweep Program Deposit Placement & Custodial Agreement, Last Updated Jan 5, 2024)
- **Mercury Treasury is a brokerage/money-market product, not a bank deposit.** Funds are invested in mutual funds (J.P. Morgan U.S. Treasury Plus MMF – JTCXX; Morgan Stanley Ultra-Short – MCRYX). SIPC-covered (up to $500K), NOT FDIC-insured, not obligations of the partner banks. (support.mercury.com/.../Understanding-Mercury-Treasury; FDIC page)

**Bottom line (conservative read):** Mercury's own documentation does NOT permit holding pooled third-party/customer funds. It affirmatively prohibits using the account "for any third parties" (Terms §1.4), disclaims acting as a money transmitter (§9), and lists "facilitating payments for third parties" as an account-closure trigger. Mercury offers standard business checking/savings (partner-bank DDAs with sweep) and a brokerage Treasury product — it does NOT offer FBO/custodial/omnibus accounts to customers. Dime Time's model (pooling end-user round-ups in one Mercury account and disbursing to third-party creditors, plus keeping the interest) appears incompatible with Mercury's Terms and would likely require a true BaaS/FBO provider plus money-transmitter/legal analysis.
